# Privacy Policy for AWS Favorites Quickbar

**Last Updated:** December 1, 2025

## Overview

AWS Favorites Quickbar is committed to protecting your privacy. This extension does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect any user data.**

The extension stores the following information **locally in your browser only**:
- Your selected favorite AWS services (service IDs like "s3", "ec2", "lambda")
- Your custom ordering of favorite services
- Display preferences (maximum number of services to show)

This data:
- ✅ Remains on your device
- ✅ Is never transmitted to any server
- ✅ Is never shared with third parties
- ✅ Is never used for analytics or tracking

## Permissions

The extension requires the following permissions:

### Storage Permission
Used to save your favorite services and preferences locally in your browser so they persist across sessions.

### Host Permission (*.console.aws.amazon.com)
Required to inject the customized favorites bar into AWS Console pages. The extension only runs on AWS Console pages and does not access any other websites.

## Third-Party Services

This extension does not use any third-party services, analytics, or tracking tools.

## Data Security

All data is stored locally using your browser's secure storage APIs. We do not have access to your data.

## Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
